Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers the Kintsugi Wonders upload tool ("the app"), a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @KintsugiWonders to upload and publish the channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to read the signed-in channel's ID and title, upload videos and captions, and change a video's privacy status and publish time. It does not access any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish Kintsugi Wonders videos. The sign-in token is stored privately on the owner's own device or private cloud environment. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising. The app has no other users and does not collect data about viewers.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account → Third-party access.
