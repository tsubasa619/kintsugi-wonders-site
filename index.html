Kintsugi Wonders

Lost to time. Rebuilt for you.

Kintsugi Wonders is a YouTube channel that rebuilds lost and ruined landmarks of Japan and the world in CG and explains their history in English: what they looked like, how they were built, and why they vanished.

Watch on YouTube: @KintsugiWonders
